NAIROBI - Kenya's central bank cut its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points to 9.5% on Tuesday, its third cut this year, saying inflation had eased faster than expected.

The decision was widely anticipated by analysts polled before the meeting. "We see room for further easing if the shilling remains stable," the bank's governor told reporters, adding that the bank would monitor food prices closely.

Inflation slowed to 3.8% in October from 4.4% a month earlier, according to the national statistics bureau.
