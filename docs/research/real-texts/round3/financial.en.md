Central bank policymakers delivered a widely expected hawkish hold on Wednesday, keeping the benchmark policy rate unchanged while pushing back against bets on aggressive easing next quarter. Futures markets had already priced in fewer than two 25-basis-point cuts before year-end, leaving the two-year yield slightly higher and nudging the yield curve flatter.

Meanwhile, Northgate Cloud reported third-quarter revenue of $4.8 billion, up 14.2% year over year, though management flagged persistent macroeconomic headwinds across European enterprise renewals. Operating margins narrowed by 110 basis points to 18.6% due to upfront data-center capital expenditure. Free cash flow nonetheless reached $920 million, comfortably above consensus estimates. Analysts noted that unless enterprise IT budgets recover more briskly than 3.4% annually, fourth-quarter guidance may invite renewed valuation pressure across mid-cap software names.
