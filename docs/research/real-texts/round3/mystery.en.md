At 6:15 a.m., the east gallery was still locked from the inside. Nobody had heard glass break, and neither the steel shutter nor the motion sensor on the north corridor had been triggered after midnight.

Inspector Razavi set his notebook on the walnut desk. "Walk me through it again, Dr. Kiani. Who beside yourself holds an override code?"

Dr. Kiani hesitated—only for half a beat, yet long enough to register. "Only the night guard on duty, and the director in emergencies. Look, I was at home until five-thirty. I couldn't possibly have stepped foot in the wing without leaving an access badge log."

"Nobody accused you of taking the painting yourself," Razavi said quietly. "I'm asking why the backup recorder had already been wiped clean before anyone called us."

Silence settled between them while the radiator clicked in the hall.
